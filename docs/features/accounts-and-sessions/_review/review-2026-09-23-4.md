### Stage 2

| # | Finding | Cite | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| X-01 | Two passes reported this separately. It is a regression from V-03. `Reserve` reads `now` once, before the throttle checks. Under concurrency, a thread whose read is a few ticks older than another thread's stamp sees a negative elapsed time, and the new `elapsedTicks >= 0` treats that as due. The thread wins the compare-and-set, moves the stamp backwards, runs another full prune of up to 100,000 entries, and writes another ERROR ceiling line. That weakens T-01's once-per-interval throttle during the flood it guards against. Every throttle test is single-threaded. | `Api/Accounts/SlidingWindowLimiter.cs:105,210-212,229-231,252-254,284-288` | Deferred, spec §8 |

New keys stay turned away uncounted, and a capped source's `Retry-After` grows by the size of the step, until real time catches up. The V-03 test passes through that path. | `SlidingWindowLimiter.cs:157,168-169,276-282,304`; `SignInRateLimitCeilingTests.cs:219-265` | Not an issue.
